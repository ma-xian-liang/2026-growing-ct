Core Thesis: Redshift as Light-Speed Expansion

The paper's central argument is that cosmological redshift is not a consequence of spatial expansion, but rather the direct manifestation of a time-growing speed of light \( c(t) \). The universe's "expansion" is, at its most fundamental level, an "expansion of the speed of light itself."

Main Innovations

  1. This model predicts a cosmic timescale of \([-45.2, -59.1]\) Gyr, far exceeding the standard 13.8 Gyr, portraying a "gentle evolution" rather than a violent Big Bang.

  2.   3. Introduction of the LEM-SCDF Concept: To resolve the profound "causal deficit" of standard cosmology—how a free photon "knows" to redshift as the universe expands—the paper posits a superluminal, globally synchronized field: the Light-Electricity-Magnetism Synchronized Cosmic Dark Field (LEM-SCDF). It argues that QCD confinement is not energy storage, but a "substance-generating process." Matter is formed irreversibly at a low "energy-substance exchange rate" (low \( c \)), and this substance is conserved, while energy is not.

Problems It Seeks to Resolve

The model attempts to provide a unified reinterpretation of several major contemporary cosmological tensions with a single, parsimonious framework:

  1. The DESI DR2 Dynamical Dark Energy Preference: The observed variation in the dark energy equation of state is interpreted as a misidentification. It is not a mysterious "quintom" fluid, but the natural consequence of \( G(t) \propto c(t)^{-2} \) altering the expansion history.

  4. Population III Star Timescale Conflict: The model predicts that lower stellar masses in the early universe lead to a dramatically slower nuclear burning rate, extending the lifetime of first-generation stars from millions to hundreds of millions of years, allowing sufficient time for galaxy assembly.

  5. Early Solar Lithium Abundance Puzzle: With the Solar System forming at \(1+z\approx1.43\), the smaller Bohr radius (scaling as \( c \)) makes light elements like lithium burn more readily in the young Sun, explaining the observed low surface abundance.

  6. The Late Emergence of Intelligent Life: The paper tentatively suggests that the evolution of \( G(t) \) and \( c(t) \) may have provided the necessary macroscopic physical conditions for the emergence of complex life on Earth.

A Brief Overall Assessment

This is a work of singular ambition and intellectual courage. Its principal strength lies in its parsimony and logical self-consistency: two postulates (invariant \( P \) and invariant \( \alpha \)) are sufficient to re-interpret a wide range of cosmological anomalies without invoking dark energy, inflation, or unobserved particles.

However, its framework is necessarily incomplete. Should they not, it still serves as an extraordinary and imaginative thought experiment, one that dares to ask: What if the universe's constants are not the laws, but the history?
